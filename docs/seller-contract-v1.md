# Seller agreement version 1

This note is for the Cleva team. It is not part of the agreement anyone signs. A Rwandan lawyer should review the agreement before a real person signs, especially the parts about holding buyer money, personal data, and electronic signatures. Add the Kinyarwanda version in Contract settings after a native speaker has checked it.

# SELLER AGREEMENT

**Contract No:** {{contract_number}}   **Date:** {{signing_date}}

## 1. PARTIES

This Agreement is made between:

**(1) {{company_legal_name}}**, a company registered in Rwanda under
registration number {{company_registration_no}}, TIN {{company_tin}}, with
its office at {{company_address}} ("the Company", "we", "us"); and

**(2) {{seller_full_name}}**, National ID / Passport No. {{seller_id_number}},
phone {{seller_phone}}, residing in {{seller_district}}, {{seller_sector}}
("the Seller", "you").

## 2. WHAT WE DO, AND WHAT WE DO NOT DO

2.1 The Company operates a platform that helps Sellers find Buyers for their
animals and farm products ("the Goods"). We act only as an **intermediary**.

2.2 We **do not**:
  (a) slaughter, process or package any animals;
  (b) transport, load or deliver the Goods;
  (c) provide veterinary services, feed, housing or storage;
  (d) own, buy or take possession of the Goods at any time.

2.3 The Seller stays fully responsible for the Goods, including their care,
health and safety, until the Buyer or the Buyer's agent has received them
and accepted them under Clause 7.

2.4 Any slaughter, transport or other service is arranged by the Seller and
Buyer between themselves, and at their own cost and risk, unless a separate
written agreement with the Company says otherwise.

## 3. SELLER'S PROMISES

The Seller promises that:
  (a) they are the lawful owner of the Goods, or are authorised by the
      owner to sell them;
  (b) the Goods are free from any debt, dispute, claim or legal restriction;
  (c) all information given about the Goods (type, breed, age, weight,
      quantity, health, location) is true and not misleading;
  (d) the Goods are healthy, and are not under quarantine, movement
      restriction or disease control, and the Seller will obtain every
      certificate, permit or movement document required by Rwandan law;
  (e) the information and documents given to the Company during
      registration and KYC are true, complete and belong to the Seller;
  (f) they will respond to Buyers and to the Company promptly and
      respectfully, and will keep Goods available once a sale is confirmed.

## 4. LISTING AND PRICES

4.1 The Seller sets or agrees the asking price for each listing. The Company
may suggest prices but is not responsible for what a Buyer is willing to pay.

4.2 The Company does not guarantee that any Goods will be sold, or sold by
a certain date or at a certain price.

4.3 The Company may refuse, edit or remove any listing that is inaccurate,
unlawful, unsafe or harmful to the platform's reputation.

## 5. CONFIRMING A SALE

5.1 A sale is **confirmed** only when (i) the Buyer and Seller agree on the
Goods, price, quantity, and delivery date and place, and (ii) the Buyer has
paid the full amount to the Company.

5.2 After confirmation the Seller may not sell the same Goods to another
person, nor raise the agreed price.

## 6. PAYMENT FLOW

6.1 The Buyer pays the full price **to the Company**, not to the Seller.
The Seller must never ask a Buyer to pay them directly for a sale arranged
through the platform.

6.2 The Company holds the Buyer's payment until delivery is completed and
accepted under Clause 7.

6.3 Within {{payout_days}} business days after delivery is accepted, the
Company pays the Seller the sale price **minus** the Company's commission
(Clause 8) and any amounts the Seller owes under this Agreement. Payment is
made by mobile money or bank transfer to the account the Seller registered.

6.4 The Seller is responsible for giving correct payment details. The
Company is not responsible for money sent to wrong details supplied by the
Seller.

6.5 Mobile money or bank fees on the payout, if any, are
{{payout_fee_rule}}.

## 7. DELIVERY AND ACCEPTANCE

7.1 Delivery takes place at the place and date agreed in the confirmed sale.

7.2 Delivery is **accepted** when the Buyer (or their agent) confirms they
received the Goods as described, or if the Buyer has not raised a complaint
within {{acceptance_hours}} hours after delivery.

7.3 If the Goods are not as described (wrong type, weight, quantity or
health), the Buyer may reject all or part of them. The Company will then
review the case under Clause 9.

## 8. COMMISSION

8.1 For every sale confirmed through the platform, the Seller agrees to pay
the Company a commission of **{{commission_percent}}%** of the total sale
price.

8.2 The commission is deducted from the Buyer's payment before the Seller
is paid. The Company will show the Seller the commission amount and net
payout for each sale.

8.3 The Company may change the commission rate by giving at least
{{rate_change_notice_days}} days' written notice. The new rate applies only
to sales confirmed after the notice period.

8.4 Commission is owed on every sale to a Buyer introduced by the Company,
even if the Seller later closes or changes the deal outside the platform
(see Clause 10).

## 9. DISPUTES ABOUT A SALE, REFUNDS AND DEDUCTIONS

9.1 If the Buyer rejects the Goods or complains, the Company may hold the
Seller's payment while it reviews photos, messages, delivery proof and
statements from both sides.

9.2 If the Company decides the complaint is justified, it may refund all or
part of the Buyer's payment from the money held, reduce the Seller's payout,
or recover the amount from future payouts to the Seller.

9.3 If the Seller fails to deliver without a valid reason (for example,
sells elsewhere, cancels without cause or delivers late), the Company may
refund the Buyer, charge a cancellation fee of {{cancellation_fee_rule}},
and suspend the Seller's account.

9.4 The Company's decision is final on platform matters, but does not take
away either party's right to go to court or arbitration under Clause 17.

## 10. NO GOING AROUND THE PLATFORM

For {{non_circumvention_months}} months after being introduced to a Buyer
through the platform, the Seller will not make deals with that Buyer outside
the platform to avoid paying commission. If they do, the Seller must pay the
Company the commission that would have been due, plus a penalty of
{{circumvention_penalty_rule}}.

## 11. RISK AND LIABILITY

11.1 Risk in the Goods, including death, illness, theft, loss, injury or
damage, stays with the Seller until delivery is accepted under Clause 7.

11.2 The Company is not liable for losses caused by the Seller, the Buyer,
transporters, slaughterhouses, veterinarians, weather, disease outbreaks,
government restrictions, or events outside the Company's control.

11.3 To the fullest extent allowed by law, the Company's total liability to
the Seller under this Agreement is limited to the commission received by the
Company for the specific sale that gave rise to the claim.

11.4 The Company is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, such as
lost profit or lost opportunity.

## 12. INDEMNITY

The Seller will compensate and protect the Company against any claim, loss,
fine, penalty or cost (including reasonable legal fees) that arises from:
(a) the Seller's breach of this Agreement; (b) false or misleading
information; (c) unhealthy, diseased, stolen or unlawful Goods; or
(d) any claim by a third party about ownership of the Goods.

## 13. IDENTITY CHECKS (KYC) AND PERSONAL DATA

13.1 The Seller agrees that the Company may collect and check their name, ID
number, phone number, location, photograph (selfie), signature, payment
details and documents, to verify identity, prevent fraud, process payments
and comply with the law.

13.2 The Company will keep this information secure, use it only for these
purposes, and not sell it. It may be shared only with payment providers,
authorities where required by law, and service providers working for the
Company under confidentiality duties.

13.3 The Seller has the rights given by Rwandan data protection law
{{data_protection_law_reference}}, including the right to ask for access to
or correction of their data. Requests may be sent to {{company_email}}.

13.4 The Seller consents to the Company using their selfie and signature
only to verify identity and to prove this Agreement was signed.

## 14. STARTING, ENDING AND SUSPENSION

14.1 This Agreement starts on the date it is signed by the Seller and
approved by the Company, and continues until ended.

14.2 Either side may end it by giving {{termination_notice_days}} days'
written notice (SMS, WhatsApp or email counts). Sales already confirmed must
still be completed and paid for.

14.3 The Company may suspend or end the Seller's account immediately if the
Seller gives false information, commits fraud, sells unhealthy or stolen
Goods, goes around the platform, abuses Buyers or staff, or breaks the law
or this Agreement.

14.4 Clauses on payment, commission, liability, indemnity, data, disputes
and governing law continue after this Agreement ends.

## 15. CONFIDENTIALITY AND CONDUCT

The Seller will keep Buyer details and Company pricing information
confidential, and will treat Buyers, Company staff and other Sellers with
respect. Harassment, threats and discrimination are grounds for
termination.

## 16. ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE

16.1 The Seller agrees that signing this Agreement electronically (by
accepting the terms, typing their name, drawing their signature and
confirming with a selfie) is as valid and binding as a handwritten
signature.

16.2 The Seller agrees that the Company's records (including time, date,
phone number, device information and verification code confirmations) may
be used as evidence of signing.

16.3 The Seller confirms that they have read the Agreement in a language
they understand, have had the chance to ask questions, and signed freely.

## 17. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

17.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Rwanda.

17.2 The parties will first try to settle any dispute by talking in good
faith and, if that fails, by mediation, within {{mediation_days}} days.

17.3 If still unresolved, the dispute will be handled by {{dispute_forum}}.

## 18. GENERAL

18.1 **Entire agreement:** this is the whole agreement and replaces earlier
oral or written promises about the same subject.
18.2 **Changes:** the Company may update these terms by giving
{{rate_change_notice_days}} days' notice. Continuing to use the platform
after that means the Seller accepts them.
18.3 **Separate parts:** if one clause is found invalid, the rest stays valid.
18.4 **No transfer:** the Seller may not transfer this Agreement to anyone
else. The Company may transfer it to a successor company.
18.5 **Notices:** sent to the phone number or email in the Seller's profile
and to {{company_address}} / {{company_email}} for the Company.
18.6 **Language:** the English and Kinyarwanda versions have the same
meaning. If they differ, {{prevailing_language}} prevails.
